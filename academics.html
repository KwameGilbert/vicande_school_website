The Vicande Difference

Vicande School is a distinguished educational institution dedicated to delivering high-quality education in an inclusive and nurturing environment. Committed to holistic development, the school ensures that students thrive academically, socially, and personally. Through a diverse and innovative curriculum, Vicande fosters critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration, equipping students with essential skills for future success. The school emphasizes character development, encouraging responsible citizenship. By creating a supportive atmosphere, Vicande inspires learners to reach their fullest potential and positively impact society.

Pre School & Kindergarten

Our Nursery is a unique pre-primary school for children aged 3 months to 5 years, focusing on holistic development—social, physical, emotional, and intellectual. We provide well-equipped classrooms and a library to foster a love of reading. Our mission is to offer high-quality education and childcare in a safe and inclusive environment.

Lower Primary (1-3)

Fostering early learning in a nurturing environment with age-appropriate materials, a computer lab, and library. Vision: cultivate academic foundation; Mission: promote intellectual, social, and emotional growth.

Upper Primary (4-6)

Preparing students for future challenges with advanced facilities. Vision: excellence in education and character; Mission: holistic education for academic and social success.

JHS (7-9)

Three-year program based on Ghana Education Service curriculum, preparing for BECE under WAEC supervision, guided by experienced teachers supporting academic and personal growth.
