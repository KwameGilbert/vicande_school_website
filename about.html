About Vicande School

Vicande School is a reputable educational institution committed to delivering high-quality education. It fosters an inclusive and nurturing environment where students can grow academically, socially, and personally.

Our Mission

At Vicande School, our mission is to provide a nurturing and inclusive environment where every student can achieve academic excellence and personal growth. We integrate values-based education to shape individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

We envision a dynamic learning community where every student is empowered to reach their full potential. Through excellence, inclusivity, and holistic development, we inspire a lifelong love of learning and prepare our students to make meaningful contributions.

Our History

In 1991-1993, growth led to acquisition of ten acres at Apatrapa. By 1996, we reached JHS level; in 1999 our pioneers excelled in BECE, placing us top in Kumasi Sub-Metro.

Vicande School offers a well-rounded education, fostering growth in spiritual, intellectual, physical, and emotional areas while emphasizing moral values and character development.

From the moment your family chooses Vicande School, the dedicated faculty and staff will ensure a smooth and supportive transition for your child.

Students thrive through a balance of academics, character development, and extracurricular opportunities.

Our Philosophy

Quality Education

Vicande School is dedicated to delivering high-quality education by implementing a comprehensive curriculum that addresses both academic and personal development. The school encourages students to explore their interests while developing essential skills such as critical thinking, collaboration, and creativity. Teachers focus on nurturing students’ intellectual growth through interactive learning methods, ensuring they are prepared for future academic and career challenges.

Proven Track Record

Vicande School has established a strong reputation for excellence in education, demonstrated through various achievements and milestones that highlight our commitment to student success. Our students consistently achieve high academic standards, evidenced by impressive performance on standardized tests and university entrance exams.

Enabling Environment and Professionalism

Vicande School is dedicated to creating an enabling environment that fosters learning, growth, and development for all students. Our modern classrooms, laboratories, and recreational spaces are designed to support various learning styles and activities. We promote the emotional and mental well-being of our students through counseling services, wellness programs, and extracurricular activities that encourage a balanced lifestyle and personal growth.

Our Team
